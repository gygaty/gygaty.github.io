03 / How I work

The connecting thread
is how I think.

I’m Gabrielly de Oliveira Balão. My work connects CRM, B2B growth strategy and marketing operations — translating business needs into journeys, processes and practical execution.

I’m interested in the space between a good idea and a system a team can actually use.

  1. 01

    Start with the business need.

    Understand the customer journey and the operational problem before choosing the solution.

  2. 02

    Build with the people involved.

    Connect leadership, marketing and sales so the work has a shared direction.

  3. 03

    Make the work repeatable.

    Use automation and clear processes to create capacity for thoughtful execution.

  4. 04

    Be precise about the evidence.

    Distinguish a measured signal, an observation and an estimate — and let each support the right conclusion.
